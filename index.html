Magic Mischief

A huge world of meadows, hills, ponds and forest with nine villages, bandit camps to wreck, changing weather, and secrets under the rocks. Trade with, duel or take quests from the wizards who roam it, fish the ponds, raid the abandoned house in the southwest, and stay clear of black cats.
